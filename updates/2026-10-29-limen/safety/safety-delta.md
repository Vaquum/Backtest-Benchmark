### FFD harness qualification

The retained FFD records all have the outer process outcome `external_deadline`, including current code. The 0.35-second parent deadline includes interpreter shutdown. **Every child had already flushed a complete method-result receipt before it was killed and reaped.** The native method outcomes therefore distinguish the old memory-cap failures/invalid acceptance from the current explicit rejections. The outer elapsed values are not method timings and do not demonstrate a current FFD hang. Shutdown was not timed separately. [interpret_boundary_probes.py](interpret_boundary_probes.py) extracts and checks those method receipts without importing or executing Limen.

The children had a hard additional virtual-memory allowance of 32 MiB, a CPU allowance and an outer process deadline; all were reaped. Initial default-environment attempts failed during setup because `/proc/self/statm` was unavailable, before the FFD ready handshake and before committing a result file. That was an instrumentation-environment limitation. The retained native-runtime invocations completed with exit code zero. None of this establishes a general bound for legitimate finite FFD workloads.

## New control surfaces that matter

### Observed execution costs: stronger evidence handling, bounded economic claims

The new observed-cost layer is substantial. Its history admission checks exact schemas, duplicate fill identities, canonical ordering, declared source-window membership, receipt chronology, nonfuture references, linkage to declared asset digests and finite positive quantities/notionals. That prevents several internally inconsistent histories from reaching calibration. It checks supplied normalized records and metadata; it does not authenticate the underlying source locators or establish when a trader actually received historical data. [History admission](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_history.py#L127-L184).

Canonical identities preserve types, finite numeric representations and frame schema; they reject cycles, duplicate JSON keys and nonfinite values. Reading a calibration requires an external expected digest and canonical bytes. Candidate inputs/policies are copied and sealed; comparisons and returned ledgers are checked for mutation. Publishing refuses different existing bytes through an exclusive hard-link operation. These are useful integrity properties, but that publishing operation does not add `fsync` durability or a transaction spanning all research artifacts. [Identity and publication](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_identity.py#L48-L141), [calibration read](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_history.py#L187-L209), [candidate/comparison sealing](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/observed_costs.py#L69-L111).

Missing or late evidence, thin observed support and unsupported transfers can yield an explicit unavailable result. Low effective support is a separate retrospective disposition. Rankings require all compared scenarios to be available and share partition, benchmark, quote currency, initial equity and fee/slippage constants. These checks reduce the chance that unlike scenarios are silently ranked together. [Support admission](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_statistics.py#L107-L195), [rank admission](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_statistics.py#L199-L217).

The calibrated scenario **replaces `slip_bps` only** and reruns the event engine. It retains the scenario's fees. Known economic failures are retained separately; other unexpected errors propagate. The result remains a retrospective constant-cost scenario: it does not establish queue position, partial fills, live stop execution, representative future costs, whole-order sampling completeness or a confidence interval with demonstrated coverage. [Scenario construction and errors](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_comparison.py#L174-L242), [sensitivity calculation](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_cost_statistics.py#L90-L104).

### Explicit exits and dynamic barriers

Explicit exits validate binary entries and nonmissing boolean exit masks, require positive integer lag, can cancel pending entries and require a later flat signal before rearming. The snapshot implementation schedules its rule-close row as `row + execution_lag_bars - 1`; this exact convention should be retained when comparing engines. Stops precede take profits under dual-touch evidence. Adverse stop gaps use opening price; take-profit gaps do not assume favorable improvement. The event path rejects some pre-entry/resize intrabar extrema because the supplied OHLC cannot establish their ordering. [Snapshot state transitions](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/_explicit_exit_strategy.py#L21-L81), [event barriers](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/backtest/trade_execution.py#L219-L242).

Dynamic-barrier producers must have importable source identity and readable source bytes; their outputs must preserve the original ordered membership and provide declared readiness/distances. Source hashes identify code, not its causal correctness. Dynamic barriers/allocation are restricted to fresh serial research runs and cannot be promoted. Explicit-exit declarations and evidence are rechecked on resume/replay. [Dynamic restrictions](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_dynamic_barriers.py#L45-L112), [exit evidence](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_exit_rules.py#L139-L171).

### Protected research and resume

Workers now consume frozen source snapshots whose bytes are checked before deserialization. Protected results must match actual history, project revision, source, occurrence and coordinator authority. Checkpoints validate the protected committed population; serial resume restores typed parameter evidence rather than accepting CSV inference as the original parameter type. New record-error mode remains restricted to ordinary unprotected serial search, and holdout, fold and storage integrity errors propagate. [Source snapshots](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_parallel_sources.py#L14-L50), [worker proof](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_parallel_commit.py#L31-L51), [checkpoint population](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_holdout_checkpoint.py#L134-L177), [error admission](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_round_errors.py#L50-L102).

Broader protected research support does not make final assessment universally composable. Final freezing still restricts exact native binary Sensors, fitted LogRegBinary/LightGBMBinary wrappers and ordinary dated inference; bar formation, walk-forward final inference, alternate test sources and data-dictionary extensions remain rejected there. Contract-bearing final assessment admits a single Sensor, and its policy rejects dynamic barriers and restricts funding/external execution sources. Local evidence cannot prove secrecy from the machine owner or absence of earlier external holdout exposure. [Final model/manifest admission](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_holdout_freeze.py#L163-L224), [contract-bearing selection](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_holdout_events.py#L45-L53), [event restrictions](https://github.com/Vaquum/Limen/blob/25e47866422d007da55cb19625344183599ba671/limen/experiment/_resolve_trade_policy.py#L174-L188).
